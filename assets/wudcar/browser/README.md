# Browser shortcut icons

YouTube and TikTok glyphs are from Simple Icons (CC0-1.0), retrieved 2026-10-16:
- https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/youtube.svg
- https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/tiktok.svg
- https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md

The original SVGs are retained here. Raster copies in `BrowserYouTube.imageset` and
`BrowserTikTok.imageset` use a white glyph on the service's red/black background.
Names and marks identify the linked services; WudCar remains independently branded.
Icons are bundled and require no favicon service or third-party tracking request.
