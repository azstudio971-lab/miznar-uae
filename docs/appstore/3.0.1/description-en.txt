WudCar is a media player for video and audio. Play sources you have the right to access.

PLAYER
Native playback, queue, resume, skip and phone playback speed.
M3U / XTREAM
Add your own playlists and accounts; browse groups and channel logos.
LOCAL LIBRARY AND PLAYLISTS
Import video and audio, manage favorites, create and reorder playlists.
CARPLAY
Browse Library, TV and Favorites using Apple templates. Video availability depends on your vehicle and system.
CAST / AIRPLAY
Choose an AirPlay-compatible car display or television. Google Cast is not included.
SUBTITLES AND PLAYER SETTINGS
Choose available embedded audio/subtitle tracks, import SRT/VTT for iPhone, and customize player preferences.
ADDITIONAL WEB BROWSER
Integrated web browser for website video playback, subject to each website’s support and terms.
SAFETY
Passengers only — do not watch while driving. Car video is locked when movement is detected. Location is used transiently for speed detection only. Apple and vehicle restrictions remain in effect.
NO CONTENT PROVIDED
WudCar provides, hosts and sells no content or IPTV subscriptions. You must have the necessary rights to all sources you add.
