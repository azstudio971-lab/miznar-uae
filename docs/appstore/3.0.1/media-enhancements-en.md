Name: WudCar: M3U IPTV Player & Cast
Subtitle: Video & Audio Player for Car

Promotional text: Your own media, playlists and servers in one player. Import files, connect supported media servers, and use AirPlay on compatible displays.

Description:
WudCar is a media player for video and audio. It does not include channels or provide content subscriptions.

Sources: add your M3U playlists, Xtream account, Jellyfin or Emby server, or sign in to Plex to select a server you own. Add single media URLs or your local files.

Library: import video and audio, organize folders and playlists, mark favorites and continue from your saved position.

Cast: use AirPlay with compatible receivers. The optional system screen-sharing extension relays captured frames to an existing external display. Receiver support, protected-content capture and background presentation depend on iOS and the display. Do not assume ordinary CarPlay supports mirroring.

Subtitles: select embedded tracks, import SRT/VTT, adjust subtitle appearance, or translate external subtitle text on-device using supported Apple Translation language pairs. External overlays appear on iPhone; remote AirPlay requires embedded subtitles.

CarPlay: browse Library, TV, Favorites and Cast through native templates. Video requires compatible hardware and system availability while parked; audio can continue when video is unavailable.

An integrated browser for website video is an additional feature, accessible through Car and web tools and CarPlay Favorites. Websites set their own access and playback requirements.

Safety: passengers only. Never watch while driving. Car video is restricted when movement is detected and remains subject to vehicle/system controls.

No content is supplied, hosted or sold. Add only sources you are entitled to use.

Subscription: existing monthly subscription only, AED24 in the UAE, with a3-day introductory free trial where Apple marks the account eligible. All app features are included. Prices and availability are shown by Apple before purchase. No new plans or timed feature limits.
