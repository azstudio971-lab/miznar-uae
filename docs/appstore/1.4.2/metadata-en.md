## Description
WudCar Media Player is a video and audio player for media sources you own or are authorized to access.

Add M3U/M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes accounts. Browse groups and channels and keep favorites close. Import videos and audio from your iPhone, create mixed playlists and resume unfinished local media.

On compatible CarPlay displays, browse Home, Video Library, M3U IPTV Player and Entertainment. Video availability depends on the vehicle and Apple platform support; audio playback remains available where video is unsupported.

Personalize supported subtitle and audio tracks, subtitle appearance, skip intervals, resume behavior, playlists, picture in picture and network buffering. External SRT/VTT captions for local videos are available on iPhone.

Web Player is an additional feature for supported website video on a car display while parked. Website restrictions and third-party policies apply.

For passengers only. Never watch while driving. GPS-based video locking is enabled by default. Follow local traffic rules and vehicle restrictions.
